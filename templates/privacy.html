Privacy

The DRSCCA apps are run by the Detroit Region of the Sports Car Club of America for its officials and volunteers.

What we keep. Your email address, to sign you in, and what you enter in the apps: funds requests, invoices, budgets, worker points and similar region records, with your name and contact details where a form asks for them.

Signing in with Google. Google tells the apps your email address and that it is yours. Nothing else from your Google account is read or kept.

How it is used. Only to run the region's business. It is kept in the region's Google Workspace and is not sold or shared outside the region, except where a payment or an SCCA report needs it.

Questions or a request to see or remove your details: admin@drscca.org.
